## Comparator ([`compare.py`](compare.py))

Adapted from [`../../blind-mapping-trial/compare.py`](../../blind-mapping-trial/compare.py).
Changes:

- **Alignment by designation, not page.** A reference entry and a blind entry link when their
  citations name the same section and share a top-level paragraph (or one cites the whole
  section), and difflib blocks of 4+ tokens cover at least half the shorter evidence span.
  The partner rule is the trial's: highest `cov_ref × cov_blind + name-word Jaccard`.
- **Edges translate through links, not only partners**, so an edge into a split entry still
  compares.
- **Fields added:** `definedElsewhere` (presence and target), `enabledBy` / `suspendedBy`
  (replacing `gatedBy`), and `crossReferences`. Cross-references are compared across the whole
  map, keyed by (section of the declaring entry, cited target), because the two maps put a
  pointer on different entries.
- **Non-partner links:** a blind entry that links but is nobody's partner is compared on
  `scope`, `clarity` and `ambiguity` against the reference entry it links to best. Without this
  the § 107.29(a)(1) and § 107.25(a) disagreements (rows 72–75) raise no flag.
- **Every flag must have a row** in [`resolutions.json`](resolutions.json); the script exits 1
  otherwise. It reads the reference from git at `85e416f`, so the flags are the ones raised
  against the map before it was corrected.

`python3 compare.py` writes [`results.json`](results.json) (alignment, flags, verdicts);
`python3 compare.py --table` prints the resolution table below.

## Limits

- One mapper, one slice. Where both maps share a misreading there is no flag.
- The `evidence` flags are the trial's span-coverage proxy. Each flagged pair was read for
  whether its span supports its entry; unflagged pairs were not re-read.
- Rows 76 and 82 are N pending #62. If #62 decides `crossReferences` must also carry the
  pointer, they become B.
