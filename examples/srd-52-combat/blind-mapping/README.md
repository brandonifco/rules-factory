# SRD 5.2.1 combat: blind second mapping

The procedure in [method.md](../../../docs/method.md) ("Before the map is used — a blind second
mapping", [0014](../../../docs/decisions/0014-a-map-is-checked-by-a-blind-second-mapping.md)),
applied to [`../corpus-map.json`](../corpus-map.json) as step 3 of
[#106](https://github.com/brandonifco/rules-factory/issues/106). It replaces the map's `legacy`
exemption in [`../review.json`](../review.json) with a `blind-second-mapping` review. It follows
[the backgammon blind mapping](../../hoyle-backgammon/blind-mapping/README.md) closely.

below are that material.

## Counts

Alignment: 80 of 86 reference entries link, 70 of them to distinct partners. 87 of 121 blind
entries link, and 17 of those link without being anyone's partner. The blind map is finer and wider:
it gives the Rules Glossary terms an in-scope entry uses their own out-of-scope entries (24 of its 38
out entries), splits the attack steps, the object-interaction paragraph and the controlled-mount
paragraph sentence by sentence, and gates every turn-scoped entry on combat.

Verdicts: **R** the reference is right. **B** the blind map is right; the reference is fixed.
**U** the corpus does not settle it; the reference now records the question. **N** not a
disagreement about the corpus: a split, a merge, a span choice, a different carrier for the same
out-of-slice rule, or a representation both maps read the same way.

These four terms are the record's own: [`resolutions.json`](resolutions.json) declares them under
`verdicts`, with `unsettledVerdict: "U"` naming the one that means the corpus does not settle it,
so a program reads the vocabulary out of the file rather than out of this paragraph
([0059](../../../docs/decisions/0059-one-adjudication-record-one-shape-and-the-vocabulary-is-declared-in-the-file.md)).

## Where the reference was right and the blind map was not

- **Combat is not a gate on turns** (55 rows). Above.
- **The Initiative-score option** (rows 4, 155). The blind map read the glossary's Initiative entry
  and did not record that the GM can replace the roll with a score, so its `initiative-roll` draws in
  a case where the corpus says no roll need be made.
- **"If you move heedlessly past your foes"** (row 28) introduces the trigger stated two sentences
  later and grants nothing; the glossary states the same single trigger.
- **Partial suspensions are not gates.** A controlled mount's Initiative change does not suspend the
  Initiative order (row 152), and the underwater ranged rule does not suspend normal and long range
  (row 188): each reaches one combatant or one kind of attack, and `suspendedBy` means the entry is
  unreachable.
- **Missing edges** the rules name: Bonus Actions and Reactions as sources of attacks (52, 53), long
  range in choosing a target (62), the turn's movement half (228), passing through a space before
  ending a move in it (108, 185).

## Limits: what could not be ruled out

- **The mapper knows this game.** A current model has read the 5e rules, earlier SRDs and a great
  deal of commentary on them, and very likely this document. The prompt told it to work from the
  text and to say where memory pulled; its notes name eight, and its three absence entries
  (flanking, a lost surprise turn, redirecting attacks at a mount) exist because it knew such rules
  from elsewhere. The coordinator who adjudicated knows the game too. Readings both share from
  prior knowledge would raise no flag, and no redaction of the docs can remove what is in the model.
  The same is true of the first mapper. Of all the limits this is the largest, and it is larger
  here than for a 1909 backgammon chapter.
- **Isolation is self-reported.** The session was not observed. The mapper says it read only the
  bundle. But it ran without a worktree of its own, so its process started in this coordinator's
  worktree, which held the reference map, its README and findings; its notes say an environment
  guard refused one compound shell command for naming that worktree, and that it re-read the bundle
  file with the Read tool instead. The bundle's parent, `/tmp/claude-1000`, also holds other
  sessions' scratch directories, including this coordinator's, which at that time held other
  sessions' repository material (the dumps of both maps written for adjudication came later). Nothing
  prevented a read. The mapper says it made none.
- **Replacement examples resemble the slice in shape.** The backgammon redaction's phase gates
  ("overtime begins once regulation time ends level; a player serving a penalty takes no part in
  play") are the method's own concepts and match this chapter's shapes (rounds, conditions that
  remove a turn) as much as they matched backgammon's. The worked entry's tie-break was rewritten
  because an Initiative tie is in the slice; the rewritten one is still a tie.
- **The adjudicator had read the reference.** One context read both maps, the corpus and the first
  mapping's findings and wrote every row. It is not the first map's author, but the adjudication was
  not independent. The B and U rows correct the reference against its own notes in four places
  (ties, communication, training, Difficult Terrain), which is where a reader of both maps is most
  likely to side with the blind map.
- **Some verdicts are judgements the corpus only leans on.** `mounting-cost` (B) rests on reading
  "until it is used up" as a floor; `grid-entering-square` (U) could be argued settled by the
  5-foot-segment sentence; `sides-agree-to-end` stays an assertion where the blind map treats
  agreement as a parameter (row 208, N), a line the method's gates do not draw cleanly.
- **Shared misreadings stay silent.** Where both maps agree there is no flag. Both, for example,
  treat `total-cover`'s "directly" and `falling-off`'s landing space as open.
- **Blind findings no flag carried.** Non-partner blind entries are compared on scope, clarity and
  ambiguity presence only, and edges to blind entries that link to nothing are skipped (42 of them).
  The blind map's gates on `hide-action`, `object-interaction-always-action` and
  `incapacitated-condition` over the communication and object-interaction entries were therefore not
  compared. The reference's `incapacitated-condition` note now names the partial reach; nothing else
  here resolves them.
- **Evidence rows are the span-coverage proxy.** Each flagged pair was read. Unflagged pairs were
  not re-read.
