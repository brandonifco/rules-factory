of Law Revision Counsel publishes them in Title 28 Appendix of the United States Code at release
point 119-110
(https://uscode.house.gov/download/releasepoints/us/pl/119/110/xml_usc28A@119-110.zip).

What is committed is a derivation and not the release. The OLRC serves Title 28 Appendix as one
4.7 MB document holding the Appellate, Civil and Evidence rules together, so a digest over it
would not say which bytes a quote is held to. examples/frcp-6-12-81/derive-corpus.py takes the
three `<courtRule>` elements verbatim from that document and drops the `<notes type="uscNote">`
child of each -- the Notes of the Advisory Committee on Rules and the editorial amendment notes,
which are transmitted with the rules and are not the rules. The derivation is reproducible from
the release by that script, and `hashDerivation` names it. README.md records what dropping the
notes costs.

No SPDX licence identifier states this, which is why the package's licence is this file rather
than an expression.
